中文

Offego 帮助你发现合适的人，交流想法，并发起或参加 Coffee Chat 和社区活动。以下说明对应当前原生 iOS 功能；具体购买是否开放，以 App 内和 App Store 的实际状态为准。

账号与使用

如何注册或登录？

已有账号在开始页选择“手机号登录”，使用已绑定的手机号和验证码登录，无需重新填写邀请码。新用户选择“通过邀请加入”，验证邀请码后完成手机号注册。当前原生 iOS 不提供邮箱或微信登录。

收不到验证码或无法登录怎么办？

确认手机号和区号正确，使用最新收到的短信验证码，检查垃圾短信及网络状态，并同意用户服务协议和隐私政策。倒计时结束后可重新发送；频繁请求可能被限流。仍有问题时，请提供提示文字、发生时间、手机号末四位和 App 版本。不要发送密码或验证码。

如何删除账号？

进入“我的 → 侧边栏 → 设置 → 账号安全 → 注销账号”，查看注销条件并提交申请。申请有 15 天冷静期，期间可在同一页面撤回，进度以页面状态为准。注销账号不等于取消 App Store 自动续订；如有订阅，请另行管理订阅。无法进入 App 时可通过下方邮箱联系。

小O找人结果不准确怎么办？

可补充行业、角色、地域或交流目的，并检查自己确认过的画像。公开资料与云端模型输出可能有误或过时，请查看来源并核实；结果不保证对方回复、接受邀约或促成合作。

如何举报或屏蔽？

在动态详情右上角的更多菜单举报内容；在个人资料或聊天设置管理屏蔽等互动。也可在侧边栏安全中心查看举报与申诉入口，或附截图、内容链接及问题描述联系支持。

共创卡与找人次数

共创卡包括什么？

当前提供按月自动续订的共创卡，中国大陆价格为 ¥29／月，其他地区及实际应付价格以 App Store 购买确认页为准。权益包括：小O每 5 小时最多 30 轮对话，仍受五小时及月度计算量限制；站内每自然月发现 30 位不同用户，同月重复查看不重复扣次；每个订阅月含 2 次站外扩展查找，未使用的赠送次数不累计；活动发布不限次数，并可跨城市发布。后台发放的免费体验卡不赠送站外扩展次数。

如何恢复购买或取消续订？

在共创中心右上角设置中选择“恢复购买记录”或“管理或取消订阅”。请使用购买时的 Apple 账户及绑定的 Offego 账号。也可在设备“设置 → Apple 账户 → 订阅”中管理。取消自动续订通常不立即终止已购买期间的权益，最终状态以 App Store 为准。

付款后权益没有到账怎么办？

保持购买时的账号登录，在共创中心设置中恢复购买或重新核对未完成购买。请勿重复购买；仍未恢复时，提供订单编号、发生时间及 App 版本联系支持。我们不会索要 Apple 账户密码、银行卡号或验证码。

找人次数什么时候扣，失败怎样处理？

会员站外基础查询不扣站外次数；扩展查找在确认后使用 1 次，最多交付 5 位候选。失败或没有符合本次条件的候选会返还该次额度；查看已有结果不重复扣次，中断的已完成任务可在 7 天内恢复。加购次数单独保留，须在共创卡有效期内使用。

“返还次数”是退回找人额度，不是现金退款。App Store 购买退款可在共创中心设置中发起，或到 Apple 报告问题页面申请，是否批准由 Apple 决定。

English

Offego helps you discover people, exchange ideas, and start or join Coffee Chat and community events. This page describes native iOS. Purchase availability depends on the current app and App Store status.

Account and everyday use

How do I register or sign in?

Existing users choose phone sign-in on the start screen and enter their linked number and verification code; they do not need another invitation code. New users choose to join by invitation and verify an invitation code before registering by phone. Native iOS currently does not offer email or WeChat sign-in.

What if a verification code does not arrive?

Check the phone number, country code, network, and filtered messages. Use the latest SMS code and agree to the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy. Retry when the countdown ends; frequent requests may be rate-limited. For support, provide the error text, time, last four digits of the number, and app version. Never send passwords or verification codes.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Me → sidebar → Settings → Account Security → Delete Account, review the conditions, and submit a request. A 15-day cooling-off period allows you to withdraw the request on the same page; that page shows its status. Account deletion does not cancel App Store automatic renewal. Manage any subscription separately. If you cannot access the app, contact support below.

What if 小O's people-search results are inaccurate?

Add the industry, role, region, or purpose of the conversation and check your confirmed profile. Public information and cloud-model output may be incorrect or outdated. Check the linked sources; results do not guarantee a reply, a meeting, or a partnership.

How do I report or block?

Use the more menu on a post to report content, or profile and chat settings to manage interactions. The sidebar Safety Center contains reporting and appeal options. You can also email support with screenshots, a content link, and a description.

Co-creation card and search credits

What does the card include?

The current card renews automatically every month. The mainland China price is CNY 29/month; regional prices and the amount charged are shown in the App Store purchase confirmation. Benefits include up to 30 小O conversation rounds per five hours, subject to separate five-hour and monthly computation limits; discovery of 30 distinct in-app people per calendar month, without charging again for repeat views within that month; two expanded external searches per subscription month, with unused included credits not carried forward; and unlimited event publication with cross-city posting. Free experience cards issued by Offego do not include expanded external-search credits.

How do I restore a purchase or cancel renewal?

Open the settings at the top right of the Co-creation Center and choose Restore Purchases or Manage or Cancel Subscription. Use the Apple Account used to purchase and the linked Offego account. You can also use device Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions. Cancellation normally stops future renewal rather than immediately ending the paid period; the App Store status applies.

What if my benefits do not appear after payment?

Stay signed in to the purchasing account, then restore or reconcile unfinished purchases from Co-creation Center settings. Avoid buying again. If the issue remains, contact support with the order identifier, time, and app version. We never need your Apple Account password, payment-card number, or verification codes.

When are search credits used or returned?

Basic external queries for members do not use an external-search credit. A confirmed expanded search uses one credit and delivers up to five candidates. A failure or no candidate meeting the current criteria returns that credit. Viewing saved results does not use another credit, and completed interrupted tasks can be recovered within seven days. Additional purchased credits are retained separately and require an active card to use.

A returned search credit is not a cash refund. You can request an App Store purchase refund in Co-creation Center settings or through Apple's Report a Problem page. Apple decides whether to approve it.

中文

Artaner 是一款把展览、旅行和日常时刻做成「邮票」收藏的 iOS App。你可以拍照或选图，套用模板，保存到本地，并使用 AI 讲解功能生成画面说明。

常见问题

Q：为什么相机不可用？
A：先检查 iPhone 设置 → Artaner → 相机 是否已开启。如果你在模拟器中测试，模拟器没有相机硬件，会显示不可用并提供从相册选图入口。
Q：长按拍摄没反应？
A：请按住至少 0.5 秒，等待快门外圈进度完成后再松开。轻点则是普通拍照。
Q：讲解功能没声音？
A：请到 iPhone 设置 → 辅助功能 → 朗读内容 → 语音，下载可用语音包。增强版或神经版语音效果更自然。
Q：讲解功能离线能用吗？
A：本地识别和模型整理可离线运行，但获取 Wikipedia 资料需要网络。没网时可能提示网络不可用。
Q：模型加载失败怎么办？
A：内置模型随 App 安装包提供。如果加载异常，建议先在设置页查看错误信息，必要时重新安装 App。
Q：数据会不会丢？
A：所有数据保存在本机。卸载 App 后数据会被删除。建议把重要内容通过导出功能备份到 iCloud Drive 或邮箱。
Q：支持哪些系统？
A：最低支持 iOS 17.0，建议使用 iPhone 12、iPad M1 或更新设备以获得更稳定的模型体验。
Q：iPad 可以用吗？
A：可以，Artaner 支持 iPad，界面会根据横竖屏自动适配。

English

Artaner is an iOS app for turning exhibits, travel, and everyday moments into collectible stamps. You can capture or import a photo, apply a template, save it locally, and use AI narration to generate contextual descriptions.

FAQ

Q: Why is the camera unavailable?
A: Check iPhone Settings → Artaner → Camera. If you are testing in the simulator, there is no camera hardware, so the app will offer photo library fallback instead.
Q: Long-press capture does not work.
A: Hold for at least 0.5 seconds and wait for the progress ring around the shutter to complete before releasing. A quick tap performs a normal capture.
Q: Narration has no audio.
A: Go to iPhone Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices and download a voice pack. Enhanced or Neural voices usually sound best.
Q: Does narration work offline?
A: On-device recognition and summarization can work offline, but fetching Wikipedia context requires internet access. Without network, the app may show a network unavailable message.
Q: What if the model fails to load?
A: The built-in model ships with the app. Check the error details in Settings first, and reinstalling the app usually fixes persistent loading issues.
Q: Will I lose my data?
A: All data is stored locally on your device. Uninstalling the app removes it. We recommend exporting important content to iCloud Drive or email for backup.
Q: Which systems are supported?
A: Minimum iOS 17.0. For the best model performance, we recommend iPhone 12, iPad M1, or newer devices.
Q: Does it work on iPad?
A: Yes. Artaner supports iPad and adapts its layout automatically for portrait and landscape use.

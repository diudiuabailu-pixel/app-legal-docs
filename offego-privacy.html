Offego（破圈）隐私政策 / Privacy Policy

最后更新 / Last updated: 2026-05-11

核心说明：Offego（破圈）是一款面向真实社交连接的社交软件，提供 Coffee Chat、资料展示、匹配连接和 AI Search 等功能。为提供服务，我们会收集实现这些功能所必需的信息，并尽力以最小化、透明和安全的方式处理你的数据。
Core summary: Offego is a social networking app designed for meaningful real-world connections, including Coffee Chat, profile display, matching, and AI Search. We collect only the information necessary to provide these features and handle it with data minimization, transparency, and security in mind.

中文

欢迎使用 Offego（破圈）。我们重视你的隐私，并致力于以清晰、透明的方式说明我们如何收集、使用、存储和保护你的个人信息。

1. 我们收集哪些信息

  • 账户与身份信息：例如昵称、头像、手机号或邮箱（如你注册时提供）、登录凭证信息。
  • 个人资料信息：例如你的职业、公司、学校、城市、兴趣标签、个人介绍，以及你主动填写在主页上的其他内容。
  • 社交与互动信息：例如你发起或参与的 Coffee Chat、连接关系、聊天互动、报名信息、反馈内容。
  • AI Search 输入内容：当你使用 AI Search 时，你输入的问题、筛选条件和相关操作记录可能会被处理，用于返回搜索结果、提升服务质量和排查故障。
  • 设备与日志信息：例如设备型号、操作系统版本、IP 地址、应用版本、崩溃日志、访问时间、网络状态等。
  • 位置信息：仅在你主动授权并使用基于地理位置的功能时收集，例如推荐附近用户、活动或城市内容。

2. 我们如何使用这些信息

  • 创建和管理你的账户
  • 展示你的资料，帮助你建立连接
  • 支持 Coffee Chat、聊天、推荐、匹配和活动相关功能
  • 处理 AI Search 请求并生成相关结果
  • 维护服务安全，防止欺诈、滥用和违规行为
  • 分析产品性能、修复故障、优化体验
  • 在你同意的情况下，向你发送服务通知、版本更新或活动消息

3. 我们如何共享信息

除以下情形外，我们不会出售你的个人信息，也不会向无关第三方披露：

  • 向其他用户展示：你公开填写的个人资料、头像、昵称、标签、公开动态等，会按产品功能展示给其他用户。
  • 服务供应商：我们可能与云服务、数据存储、消息通知、崩溃监控、安全风控等服务提供商合作，他们仅在提供服务所必需范围内处理数据。
  • 法律要求：当法律法规、司法机关或行政机关要求时，我们可能依法披露相关信息。
  • 业务变更：如发生合并、收购、资产转让等情形，相关信息可能作为交易的一部分转移，但我们会尽力确保接收方继续履行本政策中的隐私保护义务。

4. 数据存储与安全

我们会采取合理的技术和管理措施保护你的信息，包括访问控制、传输加密、日志审计和最小权限原则。但请理解，没有任何系统可以保证绝对安全。

5. 数据保留

我们会在实现本政策所述目的所必需的期限内保留你的信息，除非法律要求更长保留期。若你申请注销账户，我们会在符合法律法规要求的前提下删除或匿名化处理相关信息。

6. 你的权利

  • 访问、更新或更正你的资料
  • 删除部分内容或申请注销账户
  • 撤回授权，例如关闭定位权限、通知权限等
  • 在适用法律允许的范围内，请求复制、导出或限制处理你的个人信息

7. 未成年人保护

Offego（破圈）不面向未满 13 岁的儿童提供服务。如果你认为有未成年人未经监护人同意向我们提供了信息，请联系我们，我们会尽快处理。

8. 第三方服务

应用中可能包含第三方登录、支付、内容跳转或外部链接。相关第三方可能根据其自身隐私政策处理你的信息，请你留意相应规则。

9. 政策更新

我们可能根据产品演进、法律变化或运营需要更新本政策。重大变更将通过 App 内提示、站内通知或其他合理方式告知你。

10. 联系我们

如你对本隐私政策有任何疑问、意见或请求，请联系：service@offego.cn

English

Welcome to Offego. We value your privacy and aim to explain clearly and transparently how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information.

1. Information we collect

  • Account and identity information: such as nickname, avatar, phone number or email address (if provided), and login credentials.
  • Profile information: such as your role, company, school, city, interest tags, bio, and any other information you voluntarily add to your profile.
  • Social and interaction data: such as Coffee Chat participation, connections, chat interactions, event registrations, and feedback.
  • AI Search inputs: when you use AI Search, your queries, filters, and related usage records may be processed to return results, improve quality, and troubleshoot issues.
  • Device and log information: such as device model, operating system version, IP address, app version, crash logs, access time, and network status.
  • Location information: collected only when you actively grant permission and use location-based features, such as nearby people, events, or city-based recommendations.

2. How we use information

  • To create and manage your account
  • To display your profile and help you make connections
  • To power Coffee Chat, messaging, recommendations, matching, and event-related features
  • To process AI Search requests and return relevant results
  • To maintain security and prevent fraud, abuse, or policy violations
  • To analyze product performance, fix bugs, and improve the user experience
  • To send service notices, version updates, or event messages where permitted and/or with your consent

3. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information only in the following cases:

  • With other users: information you choose to make public, such as your profile, avatar, nickname, tags, and public activity, may be visible to other users.
  • With service providers: such as cloud hosting, storage, messaging, crash monitoring, and security providers, solely as needed to operate the service.
  • For legal reasons: when required by law, regulation, court order, or lawful government request.
  • Business transfers: in connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, or asset transfer, subject to appropriate privacy protections.

4. Data storage and security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect your information, including access controls, encrypted transmission, logging, and least-privilege practices. However, no system can be guaranteed to be absolutely secure.

5. Data retention

We retain information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy, unless a longer retention period is required by law. If you request account deletion, we will delete or anonymize relevant information subject to legal obligations.

6. Your rights

  • Access, update, or correct your information
  • Delete certain content or request account deletion
  • Withdraw permissions, such as location or notification access
  • Where permitted by applicable law, request export, copy, or restriction of processing of your personal information

7. Children's privacy

Offego is not intended for children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information without proper consent, please contact us and we will take appropriate action.

8. Third-party services

The app may include third-party login, payment, outbound links, or embedded services. Those third parties may process information under their own privacy policies.

9. Policy updates

We may update this policy as the product evolves, laws change, or operational needs shift. Material changes will be communicated through the app, in-product notices, or other reasonable means.

10. Contact

If you have any questions, comments, or requests about this privacy policy, contact us at: service@offego.cn

Shengbei Wenwen Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 15, 2026 · Version: v1.0
中文版

Shengbei Wenwen values and respects your privacy.

Shengbei Wenwen is a tool for reflection, journaling, and personal organization inspired by traditional folk culture. The App follows a local-first design, does not require account registration, and does not upload your questions, conversations, fortune texts, wishes, birth information, or photos of physical offerings to the developer’s servers.

Unless you actively use features such as model downloads, Apple subscriptions, opening external web pages, exporting backups, or sending feedback, the App’s primary content processing takes place on your device.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the Shengbei Wenwen iOS and iPadOS App, its widgets, and related public web pages.

This Policy explains:

  • what information the App processes;
  • how information is used and stored;
  • which actions may connect to Apple or other third-party services; and
  • how you can view, export, and delete your data.

2. Who We Are

Shengbei Wenwen is developed and operated by the developer identified on its App Store page.

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, our data practices, or your personal information rights, contact us at:
bailu.a@foxmail.com

3. Information Processed on Your Device

To provide the full set of features, the App may store the following content on your device.

3.1 Divination, Fortune Texts, and Conversations

This may include:

  • questions you type or enter through speech-to-text;
  • shengbei divination results, timestamps, favorite status, and related records;
  • daily fortune texts, fortune-drawing results, and interpretations;
  • your conversations with the “Interpreter”;
  • follow-up outcomes that you choose to record; and
  • recognized text from fortune-slip photos that you choose to save.

This content is stored locally by the App and is not uploaded to the developer’s servers.

3.2 Profile and Personalization Information

You may optionally provide:

  • your preferred name;
  • gender;
  • date and time of birth;
  • place of birth;
  • topics of interest; and
  • personal notes that you allow on-device interpretations to reference.

All of these fields are optional. You select your place of birth manually; the App does not use GPS to obtain your real-time location.

Only when you actively enable on-device personalization will this information be used for today’s content, traditional birth-chart displays, and on-device AI responses. It does not alter the random outcome of shengbei divination, fortune drawing, or the daily fortune.

3.3 Guardian, Wish, and Mindfulness Records

The App may store locally:

  • your currently selected guardian theme;
  • wishes and wish-completion records;
  • mindfulness and wooden-fish tapping counts;
  • in-app merit points;
  • virtual offerings, offering times, and completion records; and
  • photos of physical offerings.

This content is used only to display your own guardian, mindfulness, and review experiences. It is not used for advertising, marketing, or predicting real-world outcomes.

3.4 Settings and Usage Statistics

The App may store locally:

  • interface language;
  • daily-fortune settings;
  • notification reminder settings;
  • animation, sound, and divination appearance settings;
  • usage counts and local statistics needed for monthly reviews;
  • whether an on-device model is enabled; and
  • non-personalized status information, such as whether membership products have been successfully loaded.

This information is used to maintain App functionality, restore settings, and generate on-device review content visible only to you.

4. Camera, Photos, and Text Recognition

4.1 Fortune-Slip Photos

You may use the camera to photograph a fortune slip or select a photo through the system photo picker.

Fortune-slip photos are processed with Apple Vision for on-device text recognition:

  • the original photo is not uploaded to the developer’s servers;
  • the App does not browse your entire photo library;
  • the App can access only the photo you actively select through the system photo picker;
  • the original fortune-slip photo is not retained as a long-term historical image; and
  • recognized text may be saved as part of an on-device record only if you continue with an interpretation or conversation.

4.2 Photos of Physical Offerings

You may photograph or select a photo of a physical item to use as an offering.

Photos of physical offerings are:

  • resized and compressed on your device;
  • stored in the App’s private sandbox;
  • used for display on the offering altar;
  • displayed for 24 hours by default; and
  • removed from the offering altar and the App’s local photo directory when the offering is completed, withdrawn, or when you clear your data.

Photos of physical offerings are not uploaded to the developer’s servers and are not used for model training, advertising, or identity recognition.

5. Microphone and Speech Recognition

You may choose to enter questions or conversations by voice.

When you use voice features:

  • the App requests microphone and speech-recognition permissions;
  • speech recognition is enabled only when on-device recognition is supported;
  • recording and recognition take place on your device;
  • the App does not retain the original audio recording;
  • the original recording is not uploaded to the developer’s servers; and
  • the converted text appears in the input field and may be stored as a local record after you submit it.

You may deny microphone or speech-recognition permission without affecting keyboard input or other primary features.

6. On-Device AI and Models

Shengbei Wenwen supports AI models that run on your device for divination interpretations, fortune-text explanations, and follow-up conversations.

6.1 On-Device Inference

AI inference takes place on your device. The following content is not sent to the developer’s servers:

  • your questions;
  • conversation content;
  • fortune texts;
  • wishes;
  • birth information;
  • personal profiles; and
  • photos of physical offerings.

AI-generated content may be inaccurate, incomplete, or unsuitable for a particular situation. Please use independent judgment based on real-world information.

6.2 Model Files

Some versions of the App may include model files in the installation package.

If your installation does not include a model, you may actively download a model file from a public model-hosting service. The hosting service may receive ordinary technical information required to complete the network connection, such as your IP address, request time, and device network information. The request does not include questions, conversations, fortune texts, or profile information that you entered in the App.

Downloaded model files are stored in the App’s local sandbox and can be deleted within the App. Models bundled with the installation package are part of the App itself and can only be removed by deleting the App. Deleting a model or the App does not send any questions, conversations, or personal information to the developer.

7. Widgets and Notifications

7.1 Home Screen Widget

When you enable the daily-fortune widget, the App uses an App Group provided by Apple to share necessary local configuration and fortune data between the main App and its widget.

This content:

  • is shared only between the Shengbei Wenwen App and its widget on the same device;
  • is not uploaded to the developer’s servers; and
  • is not made available to other apps.

7.2 Local Notifications

When you actively enable daily reminders, the App requests notification permission and schedules local notifications through iOS.

Reminder settings and notification content are processed on your device and do not require push notifications from the developer’s servers. You can disable notifications at any time in the App or in system settings.

8. Subscriptions and Purchases

Shengbei Wenwen uses Apple StoreKit to provide auto-renewable subscriptions, purchases, purchase restoration, and entitlement verification.

During the purchase process:

  • Apple processes your Apple Account, payment method, billing, and transaction information;
  • the developer does not directly receive your bank card number or full payment details;
  • the App reads product names, prices, trial eligibility, and current subscription entitlements from StoreKit; and
  • the App unlocks relevant features based only on the subscription status returned by Apple.

Apple’s handling of purchase and subscription information is governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy:
https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/en-ww/

9. Backup, Import, and Sharing

9.1 Backup Files

You may actively export a local backup. A backup file may contain:

  • profile information;
  • divination records and follow-up outcomes;
  • conversations;
  • usage statistics;
  • wish, guardian, and offering records; and
  • App settings.

Backups do not include AI model files or Apple purchase credentials.

When exporting, you may use the system file panel to select “On My iPhone,” iCloud Drive, or a third-party cloud storage service. If you select an external storage service, the backup file will be handled according to that service’s privacy policy.

Backup files may contain private content. Store them securely and do not send them to people you do not trust.

9.2 Sharing Content

When you actively use the system share feature, the selected content is provided to the app or service you choose.

Share cards do not include the full text of your question by default, but you should still review the content before sending it.

The developer does not automatically share any of your records in the background.

10. User Feedback

When you actively send feedback, the App can generate diagnostic information that is fully visible to you, including:

  • App version and build number;
  • iOS version;
  • device model category;
  • system language and region;
  • App language;
  • daily-fortune setting;
  • 3D stage status; and
  • whether the on-device model is bundled, downloaded, or enabled.

Automatically generated diagnostic information does not include:

  • questions you have asked;
  • divination or conversation records;
  • fortune-text content;
  • wishes;
  • birth dates, birthplaces, or other profile information;
  • photos of physical offerings;
  • unique device identifiers such as IDFA or IDFV; or
  • real-time location.

This information leaves Shengbei Wenwen only after you confirm and send the feedback through the Mail app or another method. Once sent, the privacy policy of your chosen email or communication service applies.

11. Network Connections and Third-Party Services

Shengbei Wenwen does not operate a business server that receives users’ questions, conversations, fortune texts, wishes, or photos, and it does not integrate third-party advertising or behavioral analytics SDKs.

The App may make necessary network connections in the following circumstances:

  • retrieving product and subscription status from the Apple App Store;
  • completing a purchase or restoring purchases;
  • when you actively download an on-device model;
  • when you actively open the Privacy Policy, Terms of Use, support pages, or other external web pages;
  • when you actively send email feedback; or
  • when you actively export a backup to cloud storage or another third-party storage service.

Relevant third parties may process the technical information needed to complete a network connection in accordance with their own privacy policies:

  • Apple Privacy Policy:
    https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/en-ww/
  • Hugging Face Privacy Policy:
    https://huggingface.co/privacy
  • GitHub General Privacy Statement:
    https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement

The App does not intentionally include your questions, conversations, fortune texts, wishes, birth information, or photos of physical offerings in these requests.

12. Information We Do Not Collect or Use

Unless a future version adds a relevant feature after obtaining your explicit consent and updating this Policy, the developer does not collect through Shengbei Wenwen:

  • phone numbers;
  • contacts;
  • SMS messages or call records;
  • real-time GPS location;
  • health data;
  • bank card numbers;
  • IDFA or other advertising identifiers used for cross-app tracking;
  • photo-library content that you did not actively select; or
  • user profiles used for advertising or marketing.

We do not:

  • sell your personal information;
  • use your questions, conversations, or photos for advertising;
  • provide your content to data brokers;
  • use your private content to train public AI models; or
  • track you across apps or websites operated by different companies.

13. Data Retention and Security

User content is primarily stored in the App’s private sandbox, UserDefaults, local file directories, and App Group, and is protected by iOS application-isolation mechanisms.

We retain data only as needed for the relevant features:

  • divination records, conversations, wishes, and profile information are retained until you delete them;
  • photos of physical offerings are deleted when the offering is completed, you withdraw it, or you clear your data;
  • original photos used for fortune-slip recognition are not retained as long-term historical images;
  • downloaded AI model files are retained until you delete the model, while models bundled with the installation package are retained until you delete the App; and
  • Apple retains subscription records under its own policies.

No device, system, backup medium, or third-party service can be guaranteed completely secure in every circumstance. Please protect your device with a passcode and safeguard exported backup files.

14. Your Choices and Controls

You can:

  • delete individual divination records under “Records”;
  • delete or rename conversations;
  • delete wishes, offerings, and related photos;
  • disable on-device personalization;
  • delete downloaded AI models;
  • disable notification, microphone, speech-recognition, or camera permissions;
  • clear local user data under “Me”;
  • export a complete backup of your data; and
  • delete the App to remove user content from the App’s sandbox.

Deleting the App does not automatically cancel an Apple subscription. You must manage or cancel subscriptions in Apple’s system subscription settings.

15. Minors

Shengbei Wenwen is not designed specifically for children, and we do not intentionally collect minors’ personal information.

Minors should use the App with the knowledge and guidance of a parent or guardian and should avoid entering unnecessary identifying information such as a real name, contact details, home address, or school.

16. Nature of the Product and Safety Notice

Shengbei divination, fortune texts, traditional birth charts, guardian offerings, and AI responses are folk-culture experiences and tools for personal reflection and organization.

Related results and content:

  • do not guarantee future events;
  • do not represent real-world facts;
  • do not change fate or probability; and
  • do not constitute medical advice, psychological diagnosis, legal advice, investment advice, marital advice, personal-safety advice, or major financial advice.

For professional or high-risk matters, rely on real-world information and seek help from a qualified professional or a trusted person.

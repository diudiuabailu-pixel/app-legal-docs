Privacy Policy

Shengbei · 2026-10-19

Local data and backups

No account is required. Questions, moon-block and lot records, conversations, reading notes and bookmarks, visits and photos, follows, reminder preferences, profiles, AI memory, wishes and offering photos are stored locally. Existing birth details remain in old records and can be deleted; the new main flow does not request them. The app does not automatically upload or publish these private records. Exported backups are unencrypted JSON containing the text above, offering photos and bead photos, but not original visit photos, models, purchase credentials or cloud connection credentials. Store backups in a trusted location. Visit photos can be saved separately to Photos from visit details. Local pre-restore copies retain references to photo files so a failed restore can be rolled back. Device and iCloud backups follow system settings; the app has no own accounts or automatic cross-device sync. Card collections, incense attribution and local temple-verification receipts also stay on this device and are included in backups you export.

On-device models and speech

In on-device mode, the bundled Qwen3-0.6B is an existing model integrated to run on your device, not a model trained by this team. Questions and conversations are not sent to a cloud model for inference. Speech recognition requires on-device processing, with no server fallback. Optional model downloads contact the chosen hosting service, which may process connection information such as your IP address. These requests do not include your questions, conversations or profiles.

Optional Cloud AI and membership verification

Conversations use the on-device model by default. After you accept the cloud data notice and select Cloud AI, your question is sent to the ShengbeiWenwen backend on Tencent Cloud and then to DeepSeek. No server address or API key is required. Only individually selected history messages and current attachment text are included. Original photos, location, visits, private notes, profiles and AI memories are not attached automatically. After purchases, restores, subscription updates and foreground entry, the app sends an Apple-signed subscription proof and a random installation identifier to the backend. The backend checks current status with Apple and retains subscription and transaction identifiers, product, environment, expiry, status and verification time for access, restoration, renewals, refunds and shared usage limits. It does not retain the raw signed proof or receive your Apple password or payment card details. Access tokens are stored in the system Keychain; the server stores their hashes. Purchasing does not consent to sending conversations or change the default model. The backend forwards selected content to DeepSeek without saving conversation bodies or answers in its database. It retains request identifiers, state, model, token usage, keyed request digests, subscription status and notification identifiers for authorization, accounting and recovery. Deleting the app does not delete these records. DeepSeek handles submitted content under its service policies. Contact the email in this policy to request deletion of server records. Active members can use Cloud AI within the displayed usage and rate limits. Personal testing grants are managed separately. Stopping a request does not guarantee usage is returned; a new request may consume usage again. The app does not automatically switch models or resend questions after failure. A retry first checks the original request status.

Camera, photos and sharing

The camera supports on-device lot-paper recognition, offering photos and temple photos. The system photo picker provides only the items you select, without granting access to your entire library. Visit and offering photos stay local; temple-submission photos are sent to the developer only when you submit them. Visit and submission photos prepared by the app are re-encoded without original EXIF and GPS metadata. Add-only Photos permission is requested when you save a share card or choose to save visit photos to Photos. Visit cards omit private notes by default, include at most 100 characters if you opt in, and do not automatically include private photos. Check the preview before sharing. Shared copies are handled by the service you choose.

Location and local notifications

Location-while-in-use permission is requested only when you actively verify a check-in at an enabled temple or select a temple-submission pin. Sites awaiting verification do not request check-in location access. Check-in requires Precise Location and uses a single fix calculated on this device; raw coordinates are discarded after calculation. The app does not track movement, add the current location to visit records or send it to DeepSeek. You can still add a visit manually if access is denied, the fix is inaccurate, the area does not match or the site is unverified. A temple-submission map may start at your current location: check its pin before submitting. Only the pin you confirm and submit is sent with the form. Festival reminders, the daily 08:00 lot reminder and visit reflections use local notifications. System authorization is requested when you enable reminders. Preferences stay local and can be disabled in the app or system settings.

Card collection and keepsakes

Your collection stores earned cards, dates obtained, guardian attribution and qualifying incense days on this device. The deity, placement time, date and time zone are fixed when incense is placed; its completion time is recorded when it finishes. Old records without deity attribution do not retroactively earn progress. Temple cards retain only a local verification receipt ID, temple ID, verification date, site revision and rule version. Raw coordinates, movement and private visit notes are not retained in receipts. Local verification is a personal keepsake, not tamper-proof proof of attendance, and cloud AI does not decide it. Collections and receipts are included when you choose to export a backup and are merged with your local collection on import. Unrecognized site or rule receipts remain pending and do not award cards. Deleting a visit does not remove an earned card. Clear all data also removes the local collection, incense progress and verification receipts. Manage previously exported copies and system backups separately.

Lucky bracelet and photo beads

Bracelet materials, cumulative bead counts, sound and haptic preferences, photo beads and memories are stored on this device. Only photos you select from Photos or a visit are used. The app crops a 512-pixel copy locally and removes source EXIF and GPS metadata. Bead photos are not sent to cloud AI. Removing a bead leaves the original photo intact; deleting a visit does not remove its existing photo beads. Unencrypted backups you explicitly export include the bracelet design, counts, photo copies and memories. Keep these files secure. Older backups without a bracelet field leave the current bracelet unchanged. Clear all data removes the local bracelet and bead photos; manage exported copies separately.

Temple submissions and feedback

Temple submissions help verify and extend the public directory. They include the name, address, type, main deity, notes, an optional confirmed map pin and selected photos, without adding device identifiers or visit history. The current configuration uses email, which you confirm before sending. The developer receives the sender email address, email display name, message and attachments to handle the request. If you add attachments yourself in an external mail app, their metadata is controlled by you and that app; Shengbei cannot clean them again. Ordinary feedback contains the text and visible diagnostics you choose to send. Avoid unnecessary personal data. Contact the address below to request deletion of a submission; deleting local data does not recall sent email.

External services and diagnostics

Source websites, public policies, system Maps, sharing and Apple purchases or restores use the selected service under its own rules. The app does not receive payment-card details. It has no advertising tracking SDK and does not sell private records or use them for advertising. Local diagnostics help investigate issues. UserDefaults, App Group and file information support settings, widgets and model loading; runtime clocks measure inference and animation. Widgets read public calendar data and the lot you have revealed today, not visit photos or private notes. The current main flow has no cloud room-photo recognition entry and does not automatically upload old home-layout records.

Deletion and control

Delete records on their pages or clear local app data under Me → Settings. Profiles and AI memory have their own controls. Deleting the app does not recall shared copies, exports, system backups or Apple purchase records, and does not cancel subscriptions; manage these with the relevant service. A Keychain marker recording a successful product-catalog load may survive reinstallation. It contains no question or conversation text. Disconnect under Cloud connection and usage in a conversation to remove this installation's cloud credentials. Clearing local data does not recall submitted cloud content or delete server request metadata. Contact the email in this policy to request deletion of server records.

Content, contact and updates

The app provides traditional cultural reading, folk experiences and private records, not medical diagnosis or certain predictions. AI may be inaccurate and does not replace real-world information or professional advice. Minors should use the app with guardian guidance. Contact: bailu.a@foxmail.com. Policy changes will be reflected in the corresponding version and public pages.

The complete policy on this page controls.

LifeBook (the “App”) helps users organize everyday dictation, handwritten text, and photos into personal manuscripts. We recognize that your writing, voice, and photos can be more private than ordinary app data.

1. Information We Process

When you choose to use the relevant features, the App may process:

  • Text you enter or obtain through speech transcription;
  • Photos you add to a manuscript and their descriptions;
  • Your audio recordings;
  • Dates, places, people, moods, and writing rules you enter;
  • Chapters, book titles, tables of contents, and cover settings created from those materials; and
  • A custom HTTPS cloud AI service address and model preferences that you provide.

The current version does not require an account and does not include advertising, third-party analytics, or cross-app tracking SDKs. It does not access contacts, health data, payment information, or advertising identifiers.

2. How Information Is Stored

Manuscripts, source records, audio recordings, copied photos, and writing settings are stored by default in the App’s local container on your device. Depending on your system backup settings, this information may be included in backups provided by Apple.

You may export PDF, Markdown, plain text, or a complete JSON backup. After export, the copy is stored by the system share destination or third-party app you choose and is subject to that service’s privacy policy.

3. Speech Recognition

Dictation requires microphone and speech recognition permissions. The App uses speech recognition capabilities provided by Apple. Depending on the device, system version, language, and service availability, recognition may occur on the device or may be processed by Apple’s services. Data processed by Apple is governed by Apple’s applicable terms and privacy policy.

You may refuse or revoke these permissions and continue using handwritten text. Permissions can be managed in Settings on your iPhone or iPad.

4. AI-Assisted Organization

The App follows a local-first approach:

  • On supported devices and systems, an on-device model may be used to organize text;
  • When no cloud service is configured, materials are not sent to the developer’s server;
  • If you voluntarily enter and select a custom HTTPS cloud service, the App sends that service the book title, writing rules, task instructions, and the text to be organized. The current version does not send photos or audio files in these cloud requests;
  • How a custom service uses, retains, or deletes this information is determined by that service provider. Use only a service you trust and review its privacy policy.

AI-organized results are always presented as drafts and enter the finalized manuscript only after you review and edit them.

5. How We Use Information

The information above is used only to provide features you choose to use, including recording, speech transcription, chapter organization, editing, reading, calendar statistics, backup, and export. The current version does not use this content for advertising, profiling, data sales, or cross-app tracking.

6. Retention and Deletion

Local content remains on your device until you delete the relevant content, clear the App’s data, or uninstall the App. Uninstalling normally deletes the App’s local container, so export a complete backup first. Original photos selected from your system photo library are not deleted when you uninstall the App.

The App currently has no online account system, so we do not hold a user account or cloud manuscript that can be remotely deleted. If a third-party service you configure retains information, contact that provider directly to request access or deletion.

7. Information Security

We seek to minimize network transmission and require custom cloud addresses to use HTTPS. However, no local storage, network transmission, or third-party service can be guaranteed to be absolutely secure. Protect your device passcode, exported backup files, and the service addresses you enter.

8. Children’s Privacy

The App does not provide social accounts, public content, or targeted advertising, and does not actively ask users to provide age or child-status information. Minors should use the App with the guidance of a parent or guardian and avoid including unnecessary sensitive information in free text, audio recordings, or photos.

9. Policy Updates

If features, data practices, or service providers change, we will update this policy and its effective date. Material changes will be communicated appropriately in the App or on the relevant page.

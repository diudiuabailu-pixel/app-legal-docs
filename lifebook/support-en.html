Frequently Asked Questions

How do I start recording?

Tap the prominent Record button in the center of the bottom bar. You can switch among Voice, Handwriting, and Photos. When finished, first generate an organized result for review, then decide whether to continue to chapter editing.

Why did speech recognition suddenly stop?

Dictation uses Apple’s speech recognition service. A recognition session may be affected by the system, network, language, or service status. If it stops, keep the existing transcript and tap record again to continue, or switch to handwritten input. For longer content, dictating in sections makes review and editing easier.

Can I use the App without connecting AI?

Yes. Recording, the materials inbox, chapter editing, writing calendar, full-book reading, backup, and export do not depend on cloud AI. Supported systems prefer an on-device model. When no model is connected, the App clearly labels the result as “Local pre-organization (AI not connected).”

Where is my manuscript stored?

It is stored by default in the App’s local space on your device. Regularly go to Me → Privacy & Data → Export Full Backup. If you uninstall the App without a backup, the local manuscript may not be recoverable.

How do I export my book?

From the Me page, you can prepare and share PDF, Markdown, and plain-text files. Only finalized chapters are included in the book file; drafts and source materials are not mixed in.

How do I restore previous content?

Prepare a complete JSON backup exported by LifeBook, then choose Me → Privacy & Data → Restore Backup. Restoration replaces the current manuscript collection with the collection in the backup. We recommend exporting the current backup first.

How do I manage microphone, speech, and photo permissions?

Open the system Settings app and select LifeBook to manage microphone, speech recognition, photo, and other permissions. Refusing speech permissions does not affect handwritten recording.

How do I delete local data?

You can delete unwanted photos from chapters. To remove all local data, first export anything you wish to keep, then uninstall the App. Original photos in the system photo library are not deleted with the App.

Contact Us

When reporting an issue, include your device model, system version, App version, the page where the issue occurred, and screenshots that do not contain private manuscripts. Do not send complete recordings or sensitive manuscripts by ordinary email.
